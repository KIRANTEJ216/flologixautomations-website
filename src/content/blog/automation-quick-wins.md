# The 7 Automation Quick Wins for Small Teams

Most automation projects fail because they're too ambitious. Here are seven small wins that compound fast.

1. **Lead intake → CRM**: New form submission auto-creates a CRM record with enriched data.
2. **Invoice generation**: Approved deals trigger invoice creation and send the PDF.
3. **Meeting notes → tasks**: Transcribe calls, extract action items, push to your project tool.
4. **Slack → ticketing**: Convert flagged Slack messages into trackable tickets in one click.
5. **Email triage**: Classify incoming email by intent and route to the right inbox.
6. **Onboarding sequences**: New customer triggers a multi-step sequence across email, Slack, and your product.
7. **Weekly reporting**: Pull metrics from 3–5 sources, format, deliver to leadership every Monday.

Each of these takes 1–3 days to build and saves hours every week. Start with whichever you do most often by hand.
