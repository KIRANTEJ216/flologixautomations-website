# WhatsApp Automation: 5 Workflows Every Business Should Run

With over 2 billion users globally and open rates above 95%, WhatsApp is no longer optional. The challenge is scale — you can't manually message every customer. Automation closes the gap.

## 1. Order confirmations & shipping updates

Trigger a templated message the moment an order is placed and again at each shipping milestone. Customers get clarity, your support inbox stays quiet.

## 2. Appointment reminders

Send 24-hour and 1-hour reminders with a one-tap "confirm" or "reschedule" button. No-shows drop dramatically.

## 3. Lead qualification bots

A simple bot that asks 3–5 questions, scores the lead, and routes hot prospects directly to a sales rep beats 90% of contact forms.

## 4. Post-purchase feedback

Two days after delivery, ask a single question: "How was your experience?" Branch into review collection or service recovery based on the answer.

## 5. Cart abandonment

For commerce: detect carts left for 4+ hours, send a personalized message with the items and a soft incentive. Recovery rates beat email by 3–5x.

## Getting started

You'll need a WhatsApp Business API account (via Meta or a BSP) and an automation platform. Start with one workflow, measure for 30 days, then layer in the next.
